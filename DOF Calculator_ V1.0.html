Viewfinder

through-the-lens · true framing & defocus for this format / lens / distance
Three depth planes — foreground, subject, background (presets selectable in Scene planes) — are each tested against the calculated near/far limits. Inside the DoF a plane renders crisp; outside it receives a Gaussian defocus whose width equals the real blur-circle diameter that plane forms on the sensor (the point lights become true-size bokeh discs). Open up to f/1.4 and the planes separate; stop down to f/16 and they snap sharp as they enter the DoF. Set distances or switch either plane off in Scene planes.
